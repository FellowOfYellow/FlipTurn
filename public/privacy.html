FlipTurn Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

FlipTurn is an app for swim coaches to manage their team's roster, gear, competitions and workouts. It is available as a website and as an iPhone app. This policy explains what information FlipTurn handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • FlipTurn has no servers of its own. Your team's information is stored on your device and in your own Google Drive.
  • The people who make FlipTurn cannot see, access or collect your team's information.
  • FlipTurn has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells or shares information.

Information the app stores

Coaches enter the information FlipTurn keeps. This can include:

  • Swimmers: name, photo, level, grade, captain status, paddle and fin sizes, phone, email, parent or guardian name and contact details, date joined and club teams.
  • Coaches: name, photo, title, years coaching and Google account email.
  • Team: school name, gear inventory and gear lent to swimmers, and later competitions, relay and event entries, and workouts.

This information is saved on the device you use and, once you sign in, in a folder named "FlipTurn" in the head coach's Google Drive, together with a read-only Google Sheet copy of the same information. Coaches the head coach shares that folder with can see and edit it.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

FlipTurn uses Google sign-in so it can save your team's information to Google Drive. It asks for:

  • Your basic profile and email address, to show who is signed in and match you to your coach profile.
  • Access only to the Drive files FlipTurn creates or that you choose (the drive.file permission). FlipTurn cannot see any other files in your Google Drive.

The app talks directly to Google from your device. Your Google sign-in token stays on your device and is never sent to FlipTurn's makers. FlipTurn's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google's handling of your account is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Information about students

Swimmers are usually high school students, and some may be under 18. FlipTurn is meant to be used by coaches, not by students. Coaches decide what to enter and should only add information their school allows them to keep. Because the information lives in the coach's own Google Drive, the coach and school stay in control of it.

Keeping, changing and deleting information

  • Coaches can edit or remove any swimmer, coach or gear record in the app.
  • Deleting the "FlipTurn" folder from Google Drive removes the team's stored information there.
  • Signing out, or deleting the app or the website's data in your browser, removes the copy on that device.
  • You can remove FlipTurn's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Security

Information travels between your device and Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Who can see the team folder is controlled by Google Drive sharing, which the head coach manages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
